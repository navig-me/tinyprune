01

Rules, not routines

Set a policy once. TinyPrune watches the places you choose and schedules the next eligible item without running repeated disk-wide scans.

02

Safety has the last word

A scheduled item is checked again before it moves. Keep overrides, protected descendants, pause states, permissions, and volume availability always win.

03

Every action is legible

See the rule, expiry basis, and reason behind each decision. Preview broad rules first. Change your mind in the Mac Trash.
